Aryna Sabalenka's relentless power was on full display as she hit 35 winners to cruise past her rival in stunning fashion.

![Aryna Sabalenka powerful hit](/images/default.svg)

The Belarusian powerhouse gave her opponent no chance to establish rhythm, consistently hitting deep returns and unleashing thunderous serves. Sabalenka's improved fitness and mental resilience were clear indicators that she is fully focused on adding more major titles to her collection this year.

Her coach praised her focus and intensity, noting that this level of performance is exactly what they have been working towards during the off-season. Sabalenka remains a strong favorite as the tournament progresses.
